# How to Restore/Import a Demo Data

This guide explains how to import the provided demo data for WordPress using the WPVivid plugin.

- WordPress Installation
- ![WPVivid Backup Plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wpvivid-backuprestore/)
- ![The backup file](./wp-nextjs-blog.local_wpvivid-00d812d5112a8_2026-06-24-03-56_backup_all.zip)

---

## Step 1: Open WPVivid

1. Log in to your WordPress dashboard.
2. On the left menu, click **WPVivid Backup Plugin**.

---

## Step 2: Go to Backup & Restore

1. Click on the **Backup & Restore** tab.

---

## Step 3: Add the Backup File

1. Look for a button like **Upload Backup**, **Import Backup**, or **Add Backup**.
2. Click it.
3. Choose the backup file from the same folder as this instructions file.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.

---

## Step 4: Start Restoring

1. After the backup appears in the list, find it.
2. Click **Restore** next to it.
3. A confirmation message will show up.
4. Click **Restore Now**.

---

## Step 5: Wait Patiently

- Do NOT close the page.
- Do NOT refresh the browser.
- Wait until WPVivid finishes restoring everything.

This may take a few minutes.

---

## Done!
